        Signals only. Signal Desk never places trades and has no connection to any broker. Binary options carry a high risk of loss.

        Read this before you trade

        • Signals, not advice. The confluence score shows how many indicators agree. It is not the chance of winning.
        • Know the break-even. At an 85% payout you must win about 54% of trades just to break even. Most indicator setups land near 50%.
        • No broker link. Pocket Option has no public price feed. Live mode uses Binance crypto prices. Demo mode uses a price simulator, so its results say nothing about real markets. Broker OTC prices can differ from any outside feed.
        • Test first. Use the Backtest and Stats tabs, and a broker demo account, before risking money. Never trade money you cannot afford to lose.
